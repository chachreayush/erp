1. Executive Summary
DOC-36 defines the operational engine that turns the Principal relationship from a collection of master records into a continuously traceable operating position. The system must answer, for every Principal: what agreement applies, which products and customers are authorized, what stock is owned by the Principal, what stock is physically held by the C&F, what has moved, which commercial benefits are due, which claims and recoveries are pending, which costs belong to the Principal, what service revenue is earned, and whether the Principal statement reconciles to the source transactions.

1.1 Core outcomes
Principal is a first-class operating dimension across stock, batch, purchase/inward, sales, returns, schemes, claims, freight, commission and reporting.
Principal ownership is separate from physical location. The ERP can hold Principal-owned stock without treating the C&F as owner.
Historical transactions retain the agreement/version/product mapping/ownership rule that was actually used at posting time.
The principal statement is reconstructed from source transactions; it is not maintained by manual balance editing.
Claims, schemes, commissions and cost recoveries remain linked to their authoritative engines instead of being duplicated inside the principal module.
A Principal can share the same product with another Principal without cross-owner stock contamination.
The system supports both simple small-business C&F operation and high-volume multi-branch/multi-Principal operation using the same underlying architecture.
2. Purpose, Scope & Non-Goals
2.1 In scope
Principal operating agreements and effective-dated terms.
Principal product, territory, customer, warehouse and service-location mapping.
Ownership/custody policies for Principal stock.
Principal-wise stock movement and operational reconciliation.
Principal-linked schemes, free goods and extra allowances through DOC-14.
Principal claims, recoveries and reimbursement tracking.
Principal cost and freight attribution using DOC-35.
Principal commission/service revenue consumption from DOC-37.
Principal statement, stock statement, claim statement and profitability views.
Approvals, disputes, evidence, audit, permissions and sync behavior.
2.2 Not owned by DOC-36
Creating or editing the base Principal master identity — DOC-12.
Calculating official schemes and extra allowance entitlement — DOC-14.
Maintaining the stock movement ledger — DOC-16.
Creating double-entry accounting postings — DOC-08.
Bill-by-bill settlement allocation — DOC-07.
Final GST/e-invoice/e-way bill calculation — DOC-29.
Final freight rate calculation — DOC-35.
Final reporting engine or dashboard renderer — DOC-30/31.

5. Principal Agreement & Operating Terms
A Principal can have multiple agreement versions. Only one compatible agreement/version may be active for a transaction context unless the company explicitly permits multiple concurrent agreements by product/territory/channel.

5.1 Version resolution rule
Use transaction date/time and product/customer/territory scope to resolve the agreement version.
If no applicable version exists, block financial posting when the term is mandatory; otherwise allow a clearly marked “manual/exception” path requiring authorization.
A later amendment must not rewrite a prior posted transaction.
Overlapping equal-priority versions with incompatible terms create a configuration conflict and must not be auto-selected.
6. Principal Product, Territory & Customer Mapping
6.1 Product mapping

6.2 Validation examples
Do not allow a Principal product to be dispatched from a warehouse not authorized for that Principal unless an approved exception policy exists.
Do not silently switch stock owner because two Principals sell the same SKU.
If a customer is mapped to Principal A but the stock selected belongs to Principal B, the system must require explicit owner/business-rule resolution.
A product can be operationally common while ownership remains Principal-specific at batch or stock-lot level.
7. Principal Stock Ownership & Operational Custody
DOC-36 provides the Principal-facing ownership and custody interpretation of the DOC-16 stock ledger. Physical presence in a C&F warehouse does not, by itself, transfer ownership to the C&F company.

7.1 Stock statement formula

The formula is a reporting reconstruction from DOC-16 movement events. It must never be maintained by a separate editable “Principal balance quantity”.
8. Principal Inward, Outward & Movement Control

8.1 Operational event ledger
DOC-36 should create an append-only Principal operational event record for high-value or commercially material events. This is not a replacement for DOC-16 inventory movements; it is a Principal attribution/event index used to reconstruct the relationship timeline.

9. Principal Schemes, Free Goods & Allowances Integration
DOC-14 is the sole authority for scheme entitlement, official free goods and extra Principal allowances. DOC-36 provides Principal context and operational visibility.
Display official scheme and extra Principal allowance separately.
Show entitlement, consumed quantity, pending reimbursement and settlement status in the Principal cockpit.
Never convert a Principal allowance into a generic company discount.
Link each reimbursement candidate to its source entitlement movement.
Allow Principal-wise pending benefit aging and settlement reporting.

10. Principal Claims, Recoveries & Reimbursements

10.1 Claim lifecycle

Claim approval does not itself create financial settlement. The approved economic intent is passed to DOC-07/DOC-08 and the source claim remains linked until fully settled.
11. Principal Expenses, Freight & Cost Attribution
Principal economics require separation of gross cost, recoverable cost, absorbed cost and commission/service income. DOC-35 provides freight/logistics calculations; DOC-36 attributes those results to the relevant Principal relationship.

11.1 Cost bearer resolution
Resolve cost bearer from the active Principal agreement and applicable operational policy.
Allow transaction-specific approved override only when authorized.
Never infer Principal responsibility solely from physical stock ownership.
Preserve the original cost bearer at transaction finalization.
If Platform Admin bears a client cargo cost, use the DOC-35 Admin-borne architecture rather than silently transferring the expense to the client company.
12. Principal Commission / Service Revenue Integration
DOC-36 provides the Principal context needed by the future DOC-37 Commission & Recovery engine. It does not duplicate commission calculation logic.

13. Principal Reconciliation Engine
Reconciliation is the core control function of DOC-36. It compares source-derived Principal position against physical statements, Principal-issued statements or independently supplied figures.
13.1 Reconciliation layers

13.2 Reconciliation workspace
Period selector + Principal + branch + warehouse + product/batch filters.
Opening, additions, deductions and closing columns.
Matched / unmatched / partially matched lines.
Tolerance rules for quantity/value/date differences.
Variance reason codes and owner assignment.
Attach external statement/evidence reference.
Create follow-up claim or adjustment from a variance.
Approve and freeze a completed reconciliation period.
13.3 Reconciliation invariants
Every reported closing balance must be reproducible from source movements.
A reconciliation cannot alter history by directly changing a balance.
Corrections are made through source transactions or approved adjustment documents.
A completed reconciliation stores the report version, filters, source snapshot/version references and approver.
14. Principal Statement & Balance Architecture
The Principal statement is a structured business statement assembled from operational and financial sources. It must distinguish quantity, commercial value, claims, recoveries and settlement state.

14.1 Statement traceability
Every material line can drill to the originating transaction/document.
Every amount can show accounting status: not posted / posted / reversed / pending settlement.
Every quantity can show stock status and batch where relevant.
Every claim/reimbursement can show its current workflow state and settlement state separately.
15. Principal MIS, Profitability & Management Views

“Profitability” is a management view and must clearly identify whether the calculation is contribution, gross service margin, or another configured analytical definition. It must not silently replace statutory accounting profit from DOC-41/DOC-08.

18. Accounting & Settlement Integration
DOC-36 prepares Principal accounting and settlement intents. DOC-08 performs the final posting. DOC-07 performs bill-by-bill/open-item allocation.

18.1 Settlement rule
No Principal module screen may create its own allocation table that competes with DOC-07. A Principal claim or recovery can have a settlement target, but the applied payment/CN/DN status comes from DOC-07.
19. Tax & Compliance Integration
Principal tax identity, GST registration and legal attributes are sourced from authorized company/Principal master data.
Tax calculation and e-invoicing remain in DOC-29.
Principal context must be retained on tax-sensitive transactions for audit and reporting.
Cross-state/inter-state implications are resolved using transaction location/place-of-supply rules from DOC-29, not inferred from Principal location alone.
The Principal module must store tax rule/version references for explanatory drill-down where a transaction depends on a tax calculation.
20. Inventory, Batch, Expiry & Returns Integration
Principal operations are batch-aware because the same SKU may be supplied by multiple Principals and expiry/breakage claims require provenance.

21. Billing, Challan, Cargo & Freight Integration

21.1 Multi-challan / consolidated billing
When several challans for the same Principal are consolidated into one invoice, DOC-36 must preserve the source-challan-to-line-to-batch relationship so that Principal statements remain traceable at original dispatch level.

24. Permissions, Security & Data Scope

Permissions must be split by action (view/create/edit/approve/reconcile/export/override) and by data scope (company/branch/warehouse/Principal). Principal users cannot cross the client-company or Platform Admin security boundary.
25. Local-First / LAN / Cloud Synchronization
DOC-36 follows the ERP-wide local-first architecture. Local/LAN processing remains the primary operational execution environment; the transactional cloud layer is used for remote synchronization, recovery and authorized remote work. The ERP must not have multiple computers directly open the same SQLite/file database on Drive/OneDrive.
25.1 Principal sync requirements
All Principal event records have globally unique IDs and idempotency keys.
Agreement and reconciliation versions use optimistic concurrency/version checks.
Claim creation, approval and settlement intents are replay-safe.
Stock/statement caches are rebuildable from source facts.
Sync retries use exponential backoff and preserve local pending state.
Conflicts are presented to the user when semantic merging is unsafe; the system must not silently choose a Principal agreement or ownership value.
Audit events are retained across offline/online transitions.

26. Error, Edge-Case & Recovery Handling

26.1 Recovery principle
Every recoverable failure must identify the last durable state, the pending operation, the safe retry key and the user-visible next action. Recovery must never require direct database editing by an end user.
27. Audit, Evidence & Data Governance
Log Principal master context, agreement version usage, ownership overrides, claim decisions, reconciliation approvals and exports.
Store before/after values for material rule changes.
Record actor, role, company, branch, device/session, timestamp and correlation ID.
Link evidence documents using DOC-42/43 when available; retain document hashes for integrity.
Record why a manual override was used, not only what value was entered.
Principal statements store the source-version set used to generate them.
Sensitive Principal exports and cross-client administrative access are audited under DOC-33.
27.1 Data retention
Retention follows the ERP-wide legal/compliance policy and the configured company jurisdiction. Posted financial and operational evidence is not destructively deleted; archival policies must preserve reconstructability.
28. Reports & Dashboard Feeds

Dashboard policies from DOC-31 may exclude/snooze particular parties, ledgers or outstanding items for selected operational KPIs without deleting or altering the accounting/Principal source truth.
29. Small / Medium / Large Business Modes

All three modes use the same data model. UI complexity is reduced by feature/configuration flags, not by maintaining a separate simplified architecture.
30. Performance, Scalability & Data Retention
Index Principal ID, company ID, agreement date range, product ID, batch ID, warehouse ID, event time, source document and status.
Precompute Principal statement snapshots for frequent periods while retaining source references.
Use incremental event processing for dashboards rather than recalculating all history on every screen load.
Partition large event/audit datasets by company/period when DOC-45 warrants it.
Keep claim/reconciliation workflows responsive even when a Principal has millions of movement lines.
Never sacrifice source-level traceability for aggregation performance; summary tables are caches/derived facts.

32. Acceptance Criteria & Test Matrix

32.1 Definition of done
All mandatory Principal operational flows are implemented and linked to authoritative engines.
No duplicate accounting, settlement, inventory, scheme or freight engine exists inside DOC-36.
Principal stock and claims are traceable to source transactions.
Reconciliation cannot mutate historical balances directly.
Keyboard-first screens support the routine workflows.
Offline/LAN/cloud behavior is tested for duplicate, retry and conflict cases.
Small, medium and large business modes use one logical architecture.
Audit evidence is queryable for every material Principal decision.
33. Non-Negotiable Design Rules
Principal is a first-class operating dimension, never a free-text invoice field.
Principal ownership is separate from physical warehouse location.
Historical transactions retain the agreement/rule version actually used.
The Principal statement is reconstructed from source records.
DOC-14 remains the authority for schemes and Principal allowances.
DOC-16 remains the authority for stock movements and stock status.
DOC-07 remains the authority for financial settlement allocation.
DOC-08 remains the authority for accounting postings.
DOC-35 remains the authority for freight cost/recovery calculations.
Every material Principal balance/status is traceable to source records.
No silent ownership change, write-off, claim settlement or agreement override.
Client Companies cannot cross Principal or Platform Admin permission boundaries.
