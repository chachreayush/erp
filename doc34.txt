DOC-34
Cargo & Consignment Management Engine
C&F ERP Architecture & Implementation Blueprint
Version 1.0  |  Authoritative cargo/consignment layer  |  Code-free design
Purpose: define the complete cargo and consignment layer between physical dispatch and freight/cost management. DOC-34 owns consignment identity, transport movement, package/measurement details, LR/GR/POD and transit exceptions. It integrates with DOC-19 Dispatch, DOC-20 Billing, DOC-13 Transport/Vehicle Master, DOC-35 Freight, DOC-36 Principal/C&F and DOC-30/31/32/33 reporting, dashboard, workflow and audit.
Executive architecture
Critical new project requirement: Admin-funded client cargo
Where configured by the Platform/Master Admin Company, cargo and consignment costs incurred for a Client Company are economically borne by the Admin Company. DOC-34 must therefore separate physical service attribution, management visibility and accounting ownership. The same cargo movement can be visible to the Client Company as a client-level operational cost while the authoritative accounting expense is posted only to the Admin Company. No duplicate expense posting is created in the Client Company unless an approved recovery/chargeback policy is explicitly enabled.
This creates three distinct scopes: (1) Physical/Operational Owner - the client/order/dispatch for which the cargo exists; (2) Cost Bearer - Admin Company when the funding policy says Admin bears the cost; (3) Recovery/Chargeback Owner - optional configuration if the Admin later recovers some or all cost from the Client Company. This distinction is mandatory for reporting and audit.
1. Scope & Objectives
Create a first-class consignment record independent of invoice numbering.
Link one consignment to one or many invoices, challans, orders and dispatches, and link one invoice to many consignments where required.
Capture LR/GR and transporter/vehicle/driver information without duplicating master identities.
Model packages, weight, dimensions, CBM, route, origin, destination and delivery milestones.
Handle split, merge, repack, short-delivery, damage and reverse logistics events.
Provide a controlled POD lifecycle and exception workflow.
Capture planned, actual, recoverable and Admin-funded cargo costs for later consumption by DOC-35.
Provide client-level, Admin Company, and combined reporting scopes with authorization controls.
Support small-business simple mode and large-business multi-consignment/multi-leg mode.
Be keyboard-first, local-first/LAN compatible, sync-safe and fully auditable.
2. Ownership & Cost-Bearing Model
Cost-bearer rules
At company/service setup, the Admin Company may enable “Admin bears cargo/consignment cost” for selected Client Companies, branches, customers, principals, routes or service types.
When enabled, DOC-34 marks the consignment cost bearer as Admin Company and records the related Client Company as the operational beneficiary.
The Client Company can see the allocated cargo cost in authorized operational/MIS views, but the client ledger is not debited automatically.
The Admin Company records the expense through the configured accounting/freight process. DOC-08 remains the only posting authority.
If cost recovery is enabled, the system creates a recoverable/chargeback candidate for DOC-35 and/or the appropriate billing/accounting workflow. Recovery never changes the historical gross cost.
Every cost record stores gross cost, recoverable amount, net borne amount, beneficiary client, bearer company and recovery status so reports can reconcile without ambiguity.
3. Core Data Model
Core integrity rules
Consignment number is unique within configured company/branch/series scope.
LR/GR numbers may be unique by transporter where business practice requires it, with controlled duplicate warnings.
A package belongs to exactly one active consignment at a time; split/merge creates auditable successor/parent relationships.
Weights and CBM can be estimated and later verified; both values remain history-bearing.
One physical movement must never silently appear as two consignments without an explicit split event.
Cost records cannot be orphaned; every charge must reference a consignment, leg or approved operational cost event.
Admin-funded cost must contain both cost bearer and client beneficiary identifiers.
4. Consignment Lifecycle
Partial delivery and split consignment are modeled as controlled events, not edits to historical movement. A consignment can have multiple legs and multiple delivery outcomes.

6. Integration Architecture
7. Admin-Borne Client Cargo Cost Architecture
This section is mandatory for the user’s multi-tenant model. The Admin Company may bear cargo/consignment costs incurred for Client Companies. The system must distinguish physical beneficiary from accounting cost bearer.
7.1 Company policy setup
Default: Admin Company bears cargo cost: Yes/No
Scope: all clients / selected clients / branches / service types / routes / principals
Recovery mode: none / full / partial / contractual
Accounting destination: configured Admin expense ledger/cost centre
Analytical allocation: mandatory when client reporting is enabled
7.2 Expense recording
Every qualifying cargo charge creates an Admin-cost candidate
Capture transporter bill/charge, tax, ancillary costs, date, document reference and consignment
Mark whether cost is invoiceable/recoverable
Store client beneficiary and optional principal/customer attribution
Post only through DOC-08 after applicable approval
7.3 Client-company visibility
Client dashboard/report can show cargo cost incurred for its business
Label clearly as “Admin-borne cargo cost” or configured equivalent
Do not present the amount as a Client Company ledger expense unless chargeback is posted
Allow authorized users to drill from client report → consignment → cost document
7.4 Admin Company visibility
Admin dashboard/report shows total cargo expense borne for all clients
Breakdown by Client Company, branch, customer, principal, transporter, route, vehicle, consignment, month and cost type
Separate gross cargo cost, recoveries and net Admin-borne cost
Optional combined view across all enabled client companies
Access controlled by Platform/Admin permissions
7.5 Combined reporting toggle
Admin can enable “Include Client Company Cargo Costs” in Admin MIS
When enabled, combined dashboards/reports aggregate selected client-company cost data
When disabled, client costs remain individually visible under authorized client scopes but are excluded from Admin combined management view
Any combined report must show scope and whether figures are accounting-posted or analytical/allocated
7.6 Audit and safeguards
Every change to cost bearer/recovery policy is audited
Historical transactions retain the policy snapshot applicable at the time
No retroactive change silently rewrites prior accounting
Client visibility can be revoked without deleting cost data
Admin consolidated reporting never bypasses company isolation; it uses an authorized cross-company reporting service
8. Cargo Cost Lifecycle
Consignment created and linked to Client Company/dispatch.
Transporter/service cost captured or estimated.
System determines cost bearer from effective company policy.
If Admin-borne: record Admin expense candidate and Client operational beneficiary.
Apply DOC-35 freight calculation/recovery rules where applicable.
If recovery enabled, calculate recoverable amount separately from gross cost.
Approval is routed through DOC-32 according to amount/type/risk.
Financial posting goes only to DOC-08 and appropriate ledger/cost centre.
Client and Admin reporting receives synchronized analytical dimensions.
Final settlement/claim/recovery remains traceable to the original cost record.
Any cancellation/reversal creates a new auditable corrective event rather than deleting history.

10. Permissions & Data Scope
Client users see only their company’s consignments and authorized cost views.
Admin users with cross-company permission can view combined cargo data and Admin-borne expenses.
Cost-bearer changes require elevated permission and audit.
Editing LR/GR after dispatch requires controlled permission and may require approval.
POD evidence access follows document permissions and sensitive-data rules.
Export of combined Admin/company data is separately permission-controlled.
Users cannot change accounting ownership by editing a dashboard or report filter.
11. Keyboard-First UI
12. Local-First, Sync & Offline Behavior
Consignment creation and routine milestone entry must work on the local primary server/LAN without cloud dependency.
Each event receives a globally unique event/change ID and idempotency key.
Remote users synchronize through the existing sync architecture; no module may directly open a shared cloud file as a database.
Conflicts on non-critical fields may use configured merge rules; status, cost bearer and POD completion require explicit conflict handling where needed.
Admin-funded cost dimensions must synchronize with the same client/company context and never cross tenant boundaries without authorized reporting scope.
Failed synchronization creates a visible exception rather than silently losing cargo movement history.

14. Regional & Compliance Hooks
Transport and tax/e-document fields must be jurisdiction-configurable; India-specific GST/e-way data is consumed from DOC-29 rather than hard-coded here.
Country/region/company setup determines applicable document fields, measurement conventions, currency and statutory references.

15. Audit, Evidence & Data Quality
Log all create/edit/cancel/status/cost-bearer/recovery/POD/evidence changes.
Retain before/after values for high-risk cargo and cost fields.
Capture who, when, source device/session and company context.
Link supporting LR/GR, transporter bill, POD, damage evidence and recovery documents through the document layer.
Provide “why this cost is in Admin expense” explanation with policy, beneficiary, bearer and recovery status.
No destructive deletion after financial or dispatch significance; use reversal/amendment lifecycle.
16. Acceptance Criteria
One invoice can have multiple consignments and one consignment can link to multiple compatible sources.
Consignment can be split, merged or repacked without losing traceability.
LR/GR, package, weight, CBM, route, transporter and POD are fully traceable.
Shortage/damage/delay exceptions can be recorded and escalated.
Admin Company can be configured as cargo-cost bearer for selected Client Companies.
Admin expense reports can identify cost individually by Client Company and across all enabled companies.
Client Company can see authorized analytical cargo cost without automatically creating a client accounting expense.
Combined Admin reports can be enabled/disabled by authorization and policy.
Accounting consequences post only through DOC-08; settlement through DOC-07 where applicable.
DOC-30/31 reports and dashboards can distinguish accounting-posted vs analytical Admin-borne cost.
All significant changes are auditable through DOC-33.
Offline/LAN operation and synchronization preserve event identity and prevent duplicates.
17. Handoff to DOC-35 and Other Future Modules
DOC-34 intentionally does not own final freight pricing, recovery formulas or logistics margin. It provides movement facts and cost-source records. DOC-35 will consume these facts to calculate freight and recovery. DOC-36 may consume them for Principal/C&F operational reporting. DOC-41 can use them for closing/financial statements. DOC-42/43 will manage evidence and rendering. DOC-44–54 will harden security, database, API, sync, QA, deployment and go-live.
Architecture rule
Physical movement belongs to DOC-34; cost calculation belongs to DOC-35; accounting posting belongs to DOC-08; settlement belongs to DOC-07; reporting calculation belongs to DOC-30; dashboard presentation belongs to DOC-31; approvals belong to DOC-32; audit evidence belongs to DOC-33. The Admin-funded client cargo feature adds a cost-bearer dimension but does not create a second accounting engine.
