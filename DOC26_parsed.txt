Authoritative module specification
1. Purpose & Boundary
DOC-26 manages operational expenses and employee reimbursement processes that are not necessarily vendor purchase transactions. It covers the business reason, evidence, approval and settlement workflow, while DOC-24 remains the authoritative money-movement engine and DOC-08/09 remain the accounting authorities.
Support a simple small-business flow as well as multi-level corporate expense controls.
Do not duplicate supplier purchase, payment, settlement or accounting posting engines.
2. Expense Lifecycle
Expense Request (optional) → Advance (optional) → Expense/Claim Entry → Evidence Check → Policy Validation → Approval → Reimbursement/Payable/Accrual → DOC-24 money movement → DOC-08 posting → DOC-09 reporting.

4. Employee Advance & Settlement
Employee advances are tracked as an outstanding asset/open item until fully settled. Example: advance ₹10,000; supported expense ₹7,500; balance refundable ₹2,500. If supported expense exceeds the advance, the approved excess becomes a reimbursement payable.
Multiple advances may remain open per employee subject to policy.
Claims can be linked to one advance or multiple advances.
Unsettled advance aging must be visible and can trigger holds on new advances.
Refund of excess advance and additional reimbursement both flow through DOC-24 and DOC-07.

6. Petty Cash
Petty cash must be treated as a controlled cash account, not free-form expense notes.
Petty-cash opening balance, receipts, payments, transfers and closing count.
Cash custody by location/user where required.
Physical cash count and variance approval.
Replenishment from bank/cash through DOC-24.
Petty-cash denomination/count support can be added for operational users.
7. Approval Engine
Approval rules should be configurable by amount, expense category, employee, branch, department, cost centre and risk flags. The system should support sequential or parallel approval where appropriate.

8. Reimbursement & Payable
Approved employee claims can become a reimbursement payable. The user can pay immediately, schedule payment, or keep the approved amount outstanding according to company policy. Payment execution remains DOC-24.
One payment can settle multiple employee claims.
One claim can be settled by multiple payments when partial payment is allowed.
On-account payments and reapplication remain under DOC-07.
9. Accounting & Settlement Integration

10. Expense Numbering & Attachments
Expense claims, advances and petty-cash vouchers may each use configurable series. Series can be scoped by company/branch/financial year and must be concurrency-safe.
Supporting files, invoices, receipts, approval notes and correspondence are attached to the expense record.
Every attachment should be hashable/traceable for duplicate-detection and audit purposes.

14. Acceptance Criteria
Every approved expense has a clear owner, category, evidence status and accounting destination.
Employee advances cannot disappear through manual editing; they settle through traceable transactions.
Approved reimbursements use DOC-24 for actual payment.
Settlement uses DOC-07 rather than a second allocation engine.
Accounting posting uses DOC-08 and appears in DOC-09.
Duplicate and policy exceptions are detectable and auditable.
Petty cash supports controlled opening, movement, count and reconciliation.
The same architecture works for simple small-business use and detailed enterprise approval flows.
