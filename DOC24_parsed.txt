DOC-24
Payment, Receipt &
Cash/Bank Transaction Management Engine
Implementation Specification v1.0
Purpose: define the controlled recording, approval, numbering, allocation, reversal and reconciliation of customer receipts, vendor payments, cash movements and bank transactions while using DOC-07 as the single settlement/application authority and DOC-08/09 as the accounting/open-item authorities.
Document status
Authoritative architecture specification for money-movement transactions. DOC-24 records the financial event and payment instrument; DOC-07 controls bill-by-bill application and adjustment; DOC-08 posts approved entries; DOC-09 manages AR/AP and open items.


1. Business Purpose and Scope
DOC-24 is the operational engine for recording movement of money between the business and customers, vendors, employees, banks and internal cash locations. It does not decide which open item should be settled; that responsibility remains in DOC-07.
1.1 Core principles
Every money transaction must identify company, branch, transaction date, amount, currency and financial account.
Cash and bank movements are immutable business events after posting; corrections use controlled reversal/adjustment flows.
Allocation to invoices, bills, credit notes and debit notes is performed only through DOC-07.
A receipt/payment may be fully applied, partially applied or left on-account when policy permits.
Instrument and evidence details must be retained for auditability.
The same engine must support small businesses with simple cash/bank entry and large businesses with approvals, instruments, branches, reconciliation and maker-checker controls.

2. Transaction Model
The base money-movement document is a Payment/Receipt Transaction. A unified model should support inbound and outbound directions, multiple instruments and multiple allocation states.

2.1 Receipt vs payment
The UI should use business language: Receipt means money received by the company; Payment means money paid by the company. Transfer means movement between company-controlled cash/bank accounts and should not create AR/AP settlement by itself.
2.2 Internal transfer
Bank to bank
Bank to cash
Cash to bank
Cash location to cash location when supported
Inter-branch transfer where configured
Internal transfers require two-sided controlled entries and should be treated as transfer events, not customer/vendor settlement transactions.
3. Supported Payment & Receipt Modes

3.1 Instrument tracking
Cheque received → deposited → cleared or bounced
Cheque issued → presented → cleared or returned
Electronic receipt/payment → pending/confirmed based on bank evidence
Bounced/returned instrument must preserve original transaction and create corrective workflow, not erase history
4. Bill-by-Bill Allocation Integration
DOC-24 hands a posted money transaction to DOC-07 for application. DOC-24 must show the allocation result but must not implement duplicate allocation algorithms.

4.1 Allocation handoff
Validate party and currency compatibility before sending to DOC-07.
Pass eligible source document identifiers, amount, date and transaction context.
Receive applied/unapplied result and display it on the transaction.
Store settlement reference IDs for audit and drill-down.
Unapply/reapply requests go back through DOC-07.
5. Advance, On-Account & Unapplied Money
Not every payment or receipt is immediately tied to a bill. The system must support money that is valid but not yet allocated.

5.1 Auto-match suggestions
Exact invoice amount + same party
Reference/UTR/remittance text match
Invoice number in bank narration
Customer/vendor reference mapping
Amount/date proximity
Batch allocation of one bank receipt to several invoices
Suggestions must be explainable, non-destructive and require configured confidence/approval before auto-application.
6. Numbering & Document Series
Payment and receipt documents need controlled numbering, especially in a multi-user LAN/cloud environment. The series engine should be configurable rather than hard-coded.

Series may be scoped by company, branch, financial year and transaction type.
Next number is generated atomically; two users cannot receive the same number.
Manual override is disabled by default and requires explicit permission if enabled.
Cancelled numbers are never silently reused; gaps are auditable.
Series configuration changes are audited with old/new values.
7. Approval, Control & Security

Maker-checker for payment creation and approval.
Segregation of duties between creator, approver, posting operator and bank reconciliation reviewer for large deployments.
Role/permission checks inherited from DOC-03 and company isolation from DOC-01/02.
Sensitive bank/payment evidence is access-controlled and fully audited.
8. Bank & Cash Reconciliation
DOC-24 should include a reconciliation workbench that compares ERP transactions with external bank/cash evidence. Reconciliation is a matching process, not a second ledger.

8.1 Cash reconciliation
Opening cash

Physical cash count
Variance with reason and approval
Cash variance must not be fixed by directly editing a balance; use an approved cash adjustment transaction with reason and audit.

14. Acceptance Criteria
A user can create a receipt/payment with controlled series and unique number.
Cash, bank, electronic and cheque transactions store required mode-specific details.
A transaction can be sent to DOC-07 and show exact applied/unapplied amounts.
One payment can settle many bills and one bill can be settled by many transactions through DOC-07.
Advance/on-account amounts remain visible until applied or otherwise resolved.
Cheque/bank returns preserve the original transaction and can reopen settlement correctly.
Bank/cash reconciliation supports matching and controlled exceptions without editing source ledger balances.
Approval and segregation-of-duties rules are enforced according to configuration.
Concurrent users cannot generate duplicate payment/receipt numbers.
Every correction, reversal, allocation and reconciliation action is fully auditable.
15. Final Architecture Rule
DOC-24 is the single operational authority for recording money movement and reconciliation evidence. DOC-07 remains the single authority for applying money to open items; DOC-08 remains the accounting posting authority; DOC-09 remains the AR/AP and open-item authority. No later billing, return, claims or payment module may create a parallel payment or settlement engine.
