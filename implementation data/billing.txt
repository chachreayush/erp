=== USER'S ORIGINAL PROMPT (CONTEXT) ===

Yes. **This should definitely be part of the billing architecture of your C&F ERP.** In fact, for your type of business, I would make **Cargo / Consignment & Freight Management a dedicated section inside the billing workflow**, rather than treating freight as just another miscellaneous charge.

The important idea is that one invoice/dispatch can contain:

* Cargo/consignment information
* Transporter information
* Freight calculation
* Loading/unloading
* Other logistics expenses
* Charges recovered from customer/principal
* Actual expenses paid
* Profit/margin on the consignment
* Accounting entries
* Stock/dispatch information

And the ERP should calculate the relevant totals automatically.

# 7. D. Actual Expense vs Customer Charge

This is **very important**.

Don't mix:

> What the transporter charged us

with:

> What we charged the customer/principal.

They are not necessarily the same.

Example:

ACTUAL COST

Transporter Freight       ₹5,000
Loading                     ₹500
Unloading                   ₹300
Other                       ₹200
--------------------------------
Actual Cost               ₹6,000

But you may recover:

CUSTOMER / PRINCIPAL CHARGE

Freight                   ₹6,000
Handling                    ₹500
Other                       ₹200
--------------------------------
Recovered                 ₹6,700

Therefore:

Recovery = ₹6,700

Actual Cost = ₹6,000

Logistics Margin = ₹700

Your ERP can automatically calculate this.

# 10. Should freight be included in the invoice total?

The system should allow configuration.

For example:

### Option A — Freight taxable and billed

Products                     ₹50,000
Freight                       ₹5,000
Taxable Value                ₹55,000
GST                           ₹6,600
--------------------------------
Invoice Total                ₹61,600

### Option B — Freight separately recoverable

Product Invoice              ₹50,000
GST                           ₹6,000
Invoice Total                ₹56,000

Freight Recovery              ₹5,000

### Option C — Freight absorbed by company

Product Value                ₹50,000
GST                           ₹6,000

Customer Total               ₹56,000

Actual Freight                ₹5,000

So your billing engine should have a setting:

Freight Treatment

[ Included in Invoice ▼ ]

Options:
• Included in product price
• Added to taxable invoice
• Added as separate charge
• Non-recoverable expense
• Principal-recoverable
• Customer-recoverable

The exact tax treatment should be governed by your applicable GST configuration/rules rather than simply by this label.

=== IMPLEMENTATION PLAN / BLUEPRINT (FOR CLAUDE) ===

# Cargo & Freight Billing Architecture Specification

Claude, your task is to implement the comprehensive **Cargo/Consignment & Freight Management** subsystem inside the ERP's billing workflow.

## Phase 1: Database Updates (backend/models.py)

### 1. `Consignment` Model (New Table)
Create a new table linked to `Invoice`.
- `id`, `invoice_id` (ForeignKey)
- `consignment_no`, `lr_gr_no`, `lr_gr_date`
- `dispatch_date`, `transport_mode`, `transporter_name`
- `vehicle_no`, `driver_name`, `driver_mobile`
- `origin`, `destination`

### 2. `PackageDetails` Model (New Table)
Create a table linked to `Consignment`.
- `id`, `consignment_id` (ForeignKey)
- `package_type` (Carton, Box, etc.)
- `package_count`
- `gross_weight`, `net_weight`

### 3. `LogisticsCost` Model (New Table)
Create a table to track expenses vs recovery, linked to `Invoice`.
- `id`, `invoice_id` (ForeignKey)
- `freight_calc_method` (Weight, Fixed, Per Package, etc.)
- `actual_freight_cost`, `actual_loading_cost`, `actual_unloading_cost`, `actual_other_cost`
- `recovered_freight`, `recovered_handling`, `recovered_other`
- `freight_treatment` (Included in Invoice, Separate Charge, Non-recoverable)

## Phase 2: Frontend UI/UX (Invoice Screen)

### 1. Cargo & Consignment Panel
- Update `SalesBill.tsx` and `PurchaseBill.tsx` to include a prominent **Cargo & Consignment** panel right under the main Invoice Header or in the Additional Details tabs.
- Add fields for Consignment Details, Package Details, and Freight Calculation.

### 2. Profitability View (Role-Based)
- Add a **Logistics Profitability** summary card that calculates `Logistics Margin = (Total Recovered) - (Total Actual Cost)`.
- Restrict visibility of `actual_cost` and `Logistics Margin` to Admin roles, while Sales roles only see the `Recovered` charges.

## Phase 3: Accounting Engine Integration
- Ensure that when the Invoice is finalized, the **Posting Engine** creates distinct Journal Entries for:
  - Inventory (Stock deductions)
  - Accounts Receivable (Customer Ledger)
  - Revenue (Sales)
  - Cargo Cost Ledger (Actual freight paid to transporter)
  - Tax Records (GST)
- Keep freight expense strictly separate from product revenue in the General Ledger.
