1. Architecture & Core Principles
DOC-27 manages the complete fixed-asset lifecycle but does not become a second accounting engine. Every financial effect is posted through DOC-08 and becomes visible in DOC-09. Operational source documents remain in their originating modules.
Recommended books
Primary book: financial/book accounting basis selected by the company (for example, Ind AS/AS/local GAAP/IFRS as applicable).
Tax book(s): jurisdiction-specific depreciation and tax basis. India should support a separate Income-tax depreciation view and GST/tax-credit tracking rather than forcing tax treatment into book depreciation.
Management book(s), optional: internal management lives or reporting classes without altering statutory books.
2. Asset Master and Classification
Asset master is the durable identity record. A single asset can have multiple tags, components, locations and lifecycle events.
Parent/child assets: generator as parent, installed components as children; component replacement can retire one component without retiring the whole asset.
Capital Work in Progress (CWIP) can accumulate eligible project costs before capitalization.
Land and other non-depreciable classes can be configured as such; the engine must not assume that every asset depreciates.
3. Asset Acquisition & Capitalisation
Capitalisation begins from an approved source transaction and creates an auditable link to the purchase/inward system.
Purchase / Inward → Verification → Capital-vs-Expense decision → CWIP (if required) → Capitalisation → Asset ID → Depreciation schedule → DOC-08 posting
Capitalization wizard
1. Select source document.
2. Select asset class and jurisdiction.
3. Review invoice value, recoverable/non-recoverable tax, freight and installation amounts.
4. Choose new asset / component / CWIP / expense treatment.
5. Enter capitalization date and put-to-use date.
6. Preview book and tax treatment.
7. Post capitalization and create depreciation schedules.
4. Depreciation Engine
The depreciation engine must support independent calculation books. Book depreciation is not automatically the same as tax depreciation.
Depreciation example
Cost ₹600,000  |  Residual ₹60,000  |  Book life 5 years  |  SLM
Annual book depreciation = (600,000 - 60,000) ÷ 5 = ₹108,000
Tax depreciation is calculated separately from the India tax-book rules / applicable tax block and put-to-use conditions.
The application should display both schedules side-by-side and explain differences instead of trying to force one schedule to serve every purpose.
5. India Tax & GST Localization
India must be a first-class localization pack. The system should store tax rules by effective date and document the source/rule version used. The examples below describe architecture, not a substitute for current professional tax advice.
India source notes
Income-tax depreciation uses a tax-specific depreciation framework, including blocks of assets and WDV concepts; current notified rules/forms should drive the actual rate tables in production. Source: Income Tax Department, notified Income-tax Rules / depreciation forms (incometax.gov.in).
GST rules distinguish credit eligibility and restrictions for capital goods, including business/non-business use and taxable/exempt supply attribution; the ERP must therefore calculate and store recoverable and non-recoverable components instead of assuming full ITC. Source: CBIC GST, Input Tax Credit Rules / CGST Act materials (cbic-gst.gov.in).
MCA materials describe Schedule II as providing useful-life rules for depreciation/amortization with disclosure where a company uses a different useful life/residual value. The localization pack should therefore let the company select and document its applicable book framework. Source: Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Companies Act / Schedule II materials (mca.gov.in).
6. Global Jurisdiction Framework
The ERP should be region-aware from onboarding. Country selection does not merely change currency; it activates a jurisdiction profile that controls tax, accounting, document and reporting options.
Initial setup wizard
1. Country + state/province/emirate/territory.
2. Legal entity type and tax registrations.
3. Accounting framework and reporting basis.
4. Fiscal year and period structure.
5. Functional currency + optional secondary/reporting currencies.
6. Indirect-tax regime and registration profile.
7. Book depreciation policy and tax depreciation profile.
8. Withholding/other tax modules to activate.
9. Document compliance/local reporting modules.
10. Default asset classes, thresholds, tax blocks/classes and rules.
The setup wizard should create a region-specific default configuration but must not lock the company into a single tax policy. Authorized users can change settings; changes are effective-dated and audited.

8. Asset Maintenance, Components & Physical Verification
Maintenance history may link to DOC-26 operational expenses while keeping asset lifecycle events separate from expense claims.
Warranty/AMC references, service dates and vendor history can be stored against the asset.
Physical verification campaigns allow scan/list/count by branch, warehouse, room/site, custodian or asset class.
Statuses: Found, Found-at-wrong-location, Missing, Damaged, Under-repair, Surplus, Tag-mismatch, Pending-investigation.
Variance workflow creates approved adjustment/reclassification/write-off transactions rather than editing asset master values.
9. Embedded CA/User Guide Architecture
The final ERP must ship with an embedded, versioned guidance library for accountants, Chartered Accountants, finance users, administrators and ordinary operators. This is a product requirement, not an afterthought.
Proposed user-facing guide content
How to create an asset from a purchase bill.
How to decide capital vs expense using configured company policy and approval thresholds.
How to record GST/tax components and credit eligibility without overriding the statutory rule engine.
How book depreciation differs from tax depreciation.
How to transfer an asset between branches/custodians.
How to dispose/scrap an asset and review gain/loss.
How to correct an incorrect capitalization or depreciation schedule.
How to review the complete audit trail and supporting documents.
The guide should explicitly state when a rule is a system configuration, a company policy or a jurisdiction/statutory requirement so users do not mistake a configurable policy for law.

Integration contracts

12. Reports, Dashboards & Acceptance Criteria
Minimum acceptance tests
Create an asset from a vendor bill and preserve source traceability.
Split one purchase into multiple assets/components with correct allocated cost.
Run book depreciation and a separate tax depreciation schedule for the same asset.
Apply an effective-dated rule change without rewriting prior posted periods.
Handle transfer, impairment/revaluation (where configured), disposal and gain/loss.
Prevent duplicate capitalization when the same invoice line is reopened or synchronized twice.
India setup must support GST component capture/eligibility and separate Income-tax depreciation logic without hard-coded permanent rates, using versioned official rule sources.
Change onboarding region and verify that the active tax/depreciation/documentation profile changes while the underlying asset engine stays the same.
Open the in-ERP guide from an asset entry screen and show role-appropriate instructions plus the applicable rule version.
Final architecture decision
DOC-27 is the authoritative asset lifecycle module. It must be global by design and localized by configuration. At company creation/onboarding, the user selects the operating jurisdiction and accounting/tax profile; the ERP then loads the corresponding rule pack, forms, tax components, depreciation/tax books, document requirements and embedded guidance. Rules are effective-dated so future law changes can be introduced without redesigning the asset engine.
Regulatory note: production tax rates, useful lives, credit eligibility, withholding rules and statutory filing requirements must be maintained from authoritative jurisdiction sources and reviewed by the company’s qualified tax/accounting professional before use. The ERP should provide the rule, source/version and effective date rather than presenting unverified values as permanent law.
