=== TABLE 31 (17x2) ===
Edge case | System response
Inactive Principal used for new transaction | Block or exception-route according to company policy; never silently reactivate.
Agreement expired | Resolve historical version for existing docs; block new usage unless renewal/manual exception exists.
Two overlapping agreements | Use priority/scope if unambiguous; otherwise configuration conflict.
Same SKU supplied by multiple Principals | Require ownership dimension at selection/allocation level.
Batch has missing Principal provenance | Quarantine/exception for Principal-sensitive actions; do not guess.
Customer return cannot prove original sale | Use policy-based market-return path; mark provenance confidence and claim eligibility separately.
Claim exceeds eligible quantity | Cap eligible amount; allow residual as disputed/non-eligible with approval.
Duplicate claim | Detect by source movement/document/product/batch/reason fingerprint; block or route to review.
Principal statement disagrees with ERP | Create reconciliation variance; do not edit balances.
Principal changes mid-period | Version boundary applies by effective date; old transactions retain old version.
Warehouse transfer during disagreement | Preserve owner; hold financial/claim action if ownership unresolved.
Freight cost changes after Principal statement | Mark statement dependent on updated DOC-35 version and require controlled re-run.
Claim approved but settlement unavailable | Keep claim APPROVED / UNSETTLED; do not mark settled.
Payment allocated to wrong claim | Correct through DOC-07 unapply/reapply; preserve audit trail.
Remote conflict on reconciliation | Version conflict; user resolves after viewing both versions.
Crash during reconciliation approval | Transaction is idempotent; resume from last committed state.
